Neon Dodger Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

This policy applies to the Neon Dodger Android game (com.bugrakaya.neondodger) developed by BGR Dev. It explains what data the game handles, which third-party services are involved, and what choices you have.

In short

  • Neon Dodger is a single-player game with no accounts. Your progress stays on your device.
  • The game never asks for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or payment details, and it does not upload saves to a developer server.
  • Ads are served by Google AdMob, and consent is requested where the law requires it. What the ads SDK collects is described below.

1. Data stored on your device

Neon Dodger stores your gameplay progress and settings on your device so it can remember them between sessions, for example best score, unlocked chapters, upgrade levels, graphics settings and ad reporting preferences. A copy of this progress is also kept in a file in the game's private storage so it can be restored; it does not leave your device.

If Android backup is turned on, Android may include this data in your device backup in your Google account. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

2. Reminders

If you allow notifications, the game can schedule daily reminders on your device. They are created on the device itself: no server is involved and nothing is sent for them. The scheduled reminders are kept on the device so they can be restored after a restart.

3. Advertising and consent (Google AdMob)

The Android version uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to show rewarded and interstitial ads, and Google's User Messaging Platform to request consent where required.

According to Google's current Mobile Ads SDK disclosure, the SDK may automatically collect the following and share it with Google for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention:

  • IP address (which may be used to estimate the device's approximate location);
  • app interactions (such as ads being shown or tapped);
  • diagnostic information (performance of the app and the SDK);
  • device or other identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID and the app set ID).

You can review or change your ad privacy choices from the Privacy Options button in the main menu when it is shown. How Google uses this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (usually Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads).

4. Google Play

The game uses Google Play's in-app review and in-app update services: it may ask Google Play to show a rating prompt, and it asks Google Play whether a newer version exists. Both are handled by Google Play under Google's policies.

5. Sharing

If you use the share button, the text shown in the share sheet (your Sortie result and a link to the game on Google Play) is passed to the app you choose. Neon Dodger receives nothing back.

6. What Neon Dodger does not collect

Neon Dodger does not require account creation and does not ask you to provide personal data such as your name, email address, phone number, photos, contacts, microphone or camera access, health information or payment details. The developer does not run an account system, a cloud profile or a server for the game, and gameplay saves are never uploaded to a developer server.

7. Data sharing

The game itself sends no data to the developer. The advertising and consent SDKs described in section 3 may transmit data to Google and its partners as part of ad delivery, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention.

8. Permissions

  • Internet and network state: for ads, consent and Google Play services.
  • Notifications and running at startup: for the optional daily reminders, and to restore them after a restart.
  • Vibration: for haptic feedback during play.
  • Advertising ID: for AdMob.

9. Your choices

  • Manage your ad privacy choices from the in-app Privacy Options button when it is shown.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID and manage other privacy settings in Android settings.
  • Turn reminders off by revoking the game's notification permission in Android settings.
  • Remove locally stored game data by uninstalling the game or clearing its data.

10. Children

Neon Dodger is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

11. Turkish data protection law (KVKK)

The data controller under Turkey's Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data is BGR Dev (bugrakaya16@gmail.com). The developer processes no personal data on its own systems. The data listed in section 3 is collected automatically by the Google Mobile Ads SDK and processed by Google, including on servers outside Turkey, to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Requests about your rights under Article 11 of the law can be sent to the address above.

12. Changes

If the game's features, SDKs or data practices change, this policy is updated and its date changes. The current version is always published at this address.

13. Contact
